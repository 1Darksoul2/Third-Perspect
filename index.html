    Good to know

    How long does an order take?

    Catalog items ship in 2 to 5 working days. Custom orders depend on size and quantity, and the quote shows an estimate.

    Which files can I send?

    STL, 3MF, STEP and OBJ work best. Sketches and photos are fine too, and we can model them for an hourly fee.

    What if my part prints wrong?

    If a print has a fault that is ours, we reprint it free within 7 days of delivery.

    Do you ship across the country?

    Yes. Shipping is free on orders of ₹1,999 or more, and bulk orders can be sent by freight.
